# STILLPOINT V1.0 research

Research date: 2026-10-25. Method: official product pages, App Store and Google Play listings,
store aggregators, peer reviewed papers, WHO and Cleveland Clinic pages, and press coverage, gathered
with web search and page fetches. Prices and features change often; treat every price below as a
snapshot and recheck the store before quoting it.

**What this is not.** It is not a review of every app in the category (a 2022 content analysis found
286 store results for "pornography addiction" alone, see Henry et al. below). Reddit could not be read
directly from this environment (automated access is blocked), and no private community, Discord or
paid program was accessed. Where a cell says "not found", it means the sources reviewed did not
mention the feature, not that the product lacks it.

Legend: **✓** advertised in the cited source · **✗** source says it is not offered · **~** sources
disagree or the claim is partial · **·** not found in the sources reviewed.

**Row 23, accessibility and UX.** No store listing above documents screen reader support, reduced
motion or contrast. From screenshots and descriptions: the porn specific apps lean on dark, high
contrast "challenge" aesthetics, countdowns and red reset states; I Am Sober, Loop and Headspace use
calmer, lighter interfaces.

**Row 24, scientific claims.** Brainbuddy calls its program "evidence based"; Migiri says courses
were designed by clinical psychologists; Rebootr cites "behavioral psychology"; IronWill offers
"personalized recovery predictions" and "success probability insights" (premium); an AlternativeTo
page quotes "91% of users improved after 30 days" for Migiri with no study. None of these were
backed by a published efficacy study in the sources reviewed. This matches the only systematic look
at the category: [Henry et al. 2022, JMIR Formative Research](https://formative.jmir.org/2022/10/e39869)
reviewed 170 apps and found that none cited evidence of efficacy or safety. Their most common
features: streak tracking 42.4%, tutorials 37.1%, accountability or community 30%, blocking 27.1%.
They found no real time urge tracking tools and recommended building them.

## 3. What people say (recurring themes)

Sources: store listings and reviews, press, the studies in section 4, and nofap.com. Reddit
threads (r/NoFap, r/pornfree, r/PornAddiction) could not be fetched from here, so no thread was
read directly and nothing is quoted from them. These are anecdotal observations, not evidence.

| Theme | Evidence seen | Design response |
|---|---|---|
| Discreet name and icon | Category apps advertise "Adult Content Blocker" renames (Migiri), PIN and Face ID locks (Quitzilla, I Am Sober), "no account" trackers (Reset Ladder) | Neutral name STILLPOINT, a plain stone icon, a user chosen window title, no habit words in notifications or calendar reminders |
| Streaks without shame | Reset Ladder markets "instead of streaks"; NoFap.io sells "streak freezes"; [Prause and Binnie 2023](https://openresearch.lsbu.ac.uk/item/93513) report strong shame after relapse among forum users | Several metrics beside the streak; history never erased; neutral reset copy |
| Discouraged after a reset | Abstinence violation effect ([PMC3296289](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3296289)) | "Reset the counter. Keep the lesson." Total aligned days and longest streak survive a reset |
| Quick tool when an urge hits | Panic buttons are a headline feature (QUITTR, Fortify, Rebootr, Ever Accountable) | One button on Today opens a short action menu, no library browsing |
| Motivation fades after a few days | 90 and 100 day programs try to carry users; reviews of IronWill mention losing streaks and stopping | Small daily intentions, gentle check in, "Showed up again" badge for returning |
| Simple dashboard | Loop and I Am Sober are praised for simplicity in roundups | Today answers three questions only |
| Triggers and emotional patterns | IronWill's 16 trigger categories; Henry et al. found no real time urge tracking | Optional trigger log after each urge, insights only with enough data |
| Replace old routines | Fabulous and monk mode content focus on routines | Routine builder with five intentions max |
| Private, no account | Loop: "never sent to anyone"; a review of IronWill reports data lost on reinstall | Local first, export and import, honest warning about clearing browser data |
| Share milestones without exposure | IronWill shareable progress cards | Share card shows no habit category by default |
| Practical accountability | Ever Accountable's "I need help" pre written message | Optional trusted contact with a pre written, generic text message; no monitoring |

## 4. Scientific and medical evidence

| Topic | What the source establishes | Quality and limits |
|---|---|---|
| ICD 11 **6C72 compulsive sexual behaviour disorder** | A persistent pattern of failure to control intense, repetitive sexual impulses, over an extended period (e.g. 6 months or more), causing marked distress or significant impairment. Distress that is entirely about moral judgment is **not sufficient**. ([WHO ICD 11 browser](https://icd.who.int/browse/2024-01/mms/en#1630268048), [code summary](https://findacode.com/icd-11/code-1630268048.html)) | Authoritative definition. Frequency alone never defines it. Stillpoint does not screen or diagnose |
| Masturbation is normal | [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation): normal and healthy, linked to stress relief and sleep in studies; claimed harms are myths | Patient education page, medically reviewed 2022 |
| When to get help | [Cleveland Clinic on compulsive sexual behavior](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/22690-sex-addiction-hypersexuality-and-compulsive-sexual-behavior): seek help when behavior feels out of control and harms life areas; 988 for crisis | Used for the "talk to a professional" card |
| Treatment evidence | [Antons et al. 2022, J Behav Addict 11(3)](https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2022.00061): 24 studies, 4 RCTs; treatment seems to help, most support for CBT; specificity conclusions "with caution". A 2024 review in Archives of Sexual Behavior found mostly case reports and 4 RCTs among 28 studies | Few, small trials. Supports offering CBT and mindfulness style tools, not claiming cure |
| ACT for problematic viewing | [Crosby and Twohig 2016](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/psych_facpub/1355): 12 sessions of ACT vs waitlist, 28 men, 93% vs 21% reduction | Small, all male, mostly one religious community; therapist delivered, not an app |
| Urge surfing | [Bowen and Marlatt 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20025372/): brief mindfulness instruction did not lower urge intensity but smokers smoked fewer cigarettes over 7 days | Smokers, not this behavior; supports "urges rise and fall" framing, not a cure |
| Self monitoring | [Harkin et al. 2016, Psychological Bulletin](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/91437/): 138 studies, monitoring progress improved goal attainment (d = 0.40), more when recorded physically | Mostly health goals like diet and smoking; justifies check ins and history |
| Habit formation | [Lally et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674): automaticity plateaued after a median 66 days, range 18 to 254 (modeled) | Small sample; "21 days" and fixed timelines are myths. Goal lengths are planning choices only |
| Setbacks | Abstinence violation effect: guilt and internal blame after a lapse make further lapses likelier ([PMC3296289](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3296289)) | Theory with clinical support; drives the no shame reset design |
| Moral incongruence | [Grubbs et al., APA 2020](https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2020/02/religious-moral-porn-addiction): moral disapproval predicts feeling addicted, independent of use | Correlational; reason Stillpoint never labels users |
| Abstinence and testosterone | [Jiang et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1631/jzus.2003.0236): 28 men, a one off peak on day 7, no lasting rise; [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/increase-testosterone-45-percent/) on the misreading | Not replicated, tiny. Stillpoint makes no hormone claims |
| Apps in this category | [Henry et al. 2022](https://formative.jmir.org/2022/10/e39869): no app showed efficacy evidence | The honest position: Stillpoint is a self directed tool, not treatment |
| Forum harms | [Prause and Binnie 2023](https://openresearch.lsbu.ac.uk/item/93513): more NoFap forum engagement correlated with more depression and anxiety; shame after relapse | Correlational, author conflict of interest noted by press; still a reason to avoid a forum in V1 |
| Brain rewiring, dopamine, intelligence, masculinity, fertility, energy | No source reviewed supports claims that abstinence rewires the brain on a schedule, restores dopamine, raises intelligence, improves fertility or guarantees energy | Stillpoint does not make these claims and labels them as community beliefs in Real Talk |
| Privacy in this space | [Mozilla Privacy Not Included 2022](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/ca/privacynotincluded/articles/getting-private-mental-health-and-prayer-apps-rush-to-revise-privacy-policies/): most mental health and prayer apps reviewed earned a privacy warning | Drives local first storage |

## 5. Opportunities

1. **Separate goals for porn and masturbation.** No reviewed product clearly lets users track them
   as separate goals with separate rules; most treat "NoFap" as one combined target.
2. **Calm, shame free design.** The category's visual language is a challenge or countdown; there
   is space for a quiet, adult, well typeset product.
3. **Real time urge support and trigger logging.** Henry et al. found none; IronWill has trigger
   categories but no published method.
4. **Local first privacy without an account.** Only general trackers (Loop) and small apps (Reset
   Ladder, CleanStreak) advertise this clearly.
5. **Honest education.** Nearly every competitor implies a 90 or 100 day "reboot". Clear, sourced
   cards that separate evidence from belief are rare.

## 6. Recommended V1.0 features (built)

Onboarding with six goal choices · separate goals with their own rules (stop, or reduce with a weekly
limit) · several fair metrics · daily check in in about ten seconds · calendar that never shows
missing days as setbacks · milestone and behavior badges · urge toolkit with breathing, delay timer,
actions and trigger log · routines, focus timer, guided prompts · journal and insights with data
thresholds · Real Talk education cards with sources · local first storage in IndexedDB · export,
import, delete · milestone share card without habit details · trusted contact message · opt in
reminders as a calendar file · installable PWA with offline support.

## 7. Deliberately excluded from V1.0

| Feature | Why not now |
|---|---|
| Accounts, cloud sync, backend | Would move intimate data off the device; export and import cover device moves |
| AI coach or chatbot | Would send private text to a third party; quality and safety risks in a sensitive area |
| Public community, forum, leaderboard | Moderation burden; Prause and Binnie found harms correlated with forum engagement |
| Content blocking and device monitoring | Needs native apps or extensions and invasive permissions; best done by dedicated tools |
| Partner monitoring reports | Surveillance model; V1 keeps a user initiated message only |
| Push notifications from a server | Needs a push service and identifiers; replaced by a local calendar reminder file |
| Fixed "reboot" programs, recovery timelines | No evidence for universal timelines |
| Screening questionnaires or scores | Stillpoint is not diagnostic; frequency alone cannot define a disorder |
| Paid tiers | Out of scope; no payment provider, no gated support tools |

## 8. Privacy risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Records leave the device | No network calls for data; tests fail if `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest` or `sendBeacon` is used during core flows; strict CSP `connect-src 'self'` |
| Someone sees the app | Neutral name and icon, custom window title, share card hides the habit category, reminders say "Check in" only |
| Data loss | Clear warning that clearing browser data deletes records, export to a JSON file, import to restore, request for persistent storage |
| Corrupt or tampered storage | Every record is validated on load; bad records are skipped and the user is told how many |
| Storage unavailable (private mode) | Falls back to memory for the session with a visible notice |
| Shared device | Delete everything in two steps; no account to recover |
| Overclaiming security | The privacy page states records are **not encrypted** by Stillpoint and rely on the device's own protection |
